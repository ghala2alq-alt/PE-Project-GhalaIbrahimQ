# TC Rules V1

**Owner:** Learner Support Desk (Training Center)  
**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Version:** v1

1. **Data:** Before using a learner inquiry, replace unnecessary personal names, phone numbers, reference numbers, and identifying details with placeholders.
2. **Verification:** Before sending a reply, check every factual claim against the approved TC Facts and score the five-point checklist. Send only replies that score 5/5.
3. **Fairness:** Before approving a template, test equivalent inquiries with different names or wording, and confirm there is no unexplained difference in treatment.
4. **Policy:** Before giving procedural guidance, check the approved TC Facts and include only supported procedures and time limits.
5. **Human decision:** Before sending, avoid deciding eligibility or claiming an action or status is confirmed without authorized confirmation. Refer decisions that require human judgment to the appropriate staff member.

Each rule is checkable with **Yes / No**.
